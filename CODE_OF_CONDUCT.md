# Code of Conduct

potato box contributors must keep collaboration professional, specific, and safe.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss technical decisions and observable behavior, not personal traits.
- Give actionable feedback and explain tradeoffs.
- Respect privacy, credentials, unpublished security reports, and user data.
- Welcome good-faith questions and corrections.
- Credit work accurately and preserve required license notices.
- Stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as disruptive or unsafe.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, credential exposure, deliberate sabotage, plagiarism, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer or through the repository's private security/contact channel when confidentiality is needed. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban participants. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of reporters or affected people.

This policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, community spaces, and public representation of the project.
